QUEEN'S DOMAIN GRAPHICS 1.0.3

Close the game and choose ONE download.

STANDALONE - no existing UE4SS
Extract QueensDomain-Graphics-Standalone-1.0.3.zip into the game folder
containing QueensDomain.exe, merging the QueensDomain folder. Stable UE4SS
3.0.1 is included and configured. Do not extract over an existing UE4SS setup.

MOD-ONLY - existing UE4SS (stable or experimental)
Extract QueensDomain-Graphics-1.0.3.zip to a temporary folder.
1. Copy BOTH files inside PAKs into the game's QueensDomain/Content/Paks.
2. Copy the WHOLE QDGraphics folder into your active UE4SS Mods folder:
   Flat layout (usually stable 3.0.1):
   QueensDomain/Binaries/Win64/Mods/QDGraphics
   Nested layout (usually experimental):
   QueensDomain/Binaries/Win64/ue4ss/Mods/QDGraphics
Use the existing folder structure as your guide. Custom setups may differ.
Keep one active copy. Merge/replace QDGraphics files when updating; keep your
loader, configuration and other mods. Do not extract this ZIP into the game
root and expect automatic installation: the two folders have separate destinations.

Launch, load your save, then choose Options > Video > Graphics preset > Modern.
A working v1.0.2 installation needs no update. Graphics and settings are unchanged.

Original: stock retro resolution, AA off, game-default AF, dithering on,
original colour steps, and original vignette.
Modern: full resolution (100%), TAA, 16x AF, dithering off, Smooth + bias,
and original vignette.
Custom: your own choices; changing individual controls selects it automatically.

AA: Off, FXAA (edge smoothing), or TAA (less shimmering, potentially softer).
AF: Game default or 1x-16x; improves angled textures where sampling allows.
Dithering: toggle the patterned colour noise.
Gradients: Original (stepped), Smooth (interpolated), or Smooth + bias
(interpolated with more of the original palette's warmth).
Vignette: edge darkening, from Off to the original strength at 100%.

Fresh installs preserve your current settings until you choose an option.
Changes save immediately. Old Enhanced preferences become Modern.
The included enabled.txt enables QDGraphics without replacing mods.txt.
For a nonstandard UE4SS working directory, move QDGraphics into its Mods folder.
If your mod list explicitly disables QDGraphics, enable it there.
Standalone enables ConsoleEnablerMod: F10 opens the console. With mod-only,
enable that helper if wanted. qd_help lists commands, qd_status shows values,
and qd_defaults selects Original. The menu works without the console.

I tested stable UE4SS 3.0.1 and experimental 3.0.1-1217-g4fb9b615 on Steam
build 26496428 with DX11 and DX12 (6 November 2026). Standalone bundles stable
3.0.1. Later experimental builds need their own testing.
Updates or mods replacing the same three assets may require a rebuild.
Smooth gradients cost additional texture samples; Lua has no polling timer.

UNINSTALL (close the game first)
Remove QueensDomain_Graphics_P.pak and QueensDomain_GraphicsMenu_P.pak from
QueensDomain/Content/Paks, and QDGraphics from UE4SS's Mods folder.
Keep other mods, original game files, and saves. Choose Original before removal
to restore stock resolution behavior. Optional preference reset: delete
%LOCALAPPDATA%\QueensDomain\Saved\QDGraphics.ini and QDGraphics.ini.bak.

If standalone installed UE4SS and no other mods need it, you may also remove
dwmapi.dll, UE4SS.dll, UE4SS-settings.ini, UE4SS-LICENSE.txt,
Mods/ConsoleEnablerMod, Mods/shared/UEHelpers, and Mods/mods.txt from
QueensDomain/Binaries/Win64. Keep shared loader files if other mods use them.

TROUBLESHOOTING
Menu rows can appear even when Lua failed to load. Check that QDGraphics is in
the active Mods folder. Send UE4SS.log from the active loader folder, your
UE4SS version, and the package you installed when reporting a problem.
The usual log is QueensDomain/Binaries/Win64/UE4SS.log (flat), or
QueensDomain/Binaries/Win64/ue4ss/UE4SS.log (nested).

UPGRADING EARLY LOCAL TEST BUILDS
Disable ConsoleLab and NoDither, and remove the earlier
QueensDomain_SmoothLUT_P.pak before using this replacement.
