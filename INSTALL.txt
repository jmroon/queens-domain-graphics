QUEEN'S DOMAIN GRAPHICS 1.0.0

1. Close the game. Install basic UE4SS 3.0.1 into
   QueensDomain/Binaries/Win64, beside QueensDomain-Win64-Shipping.exe:
   https://github.com/UE4SS-RE/RE-UE4SS/releases/tag/v3.0.1
   Preserve an existing UE4SS installation and its other mods.
2. Extract this ZIP into the Steam game folder containing QueensDomain.exe.
   Merge the QueensDomain folder. UE4SS is a separate prerequisite.
3. Launch, load your save, then choose Options > Video > Graphics preset > Modern.

Original: stock retro resolution, AA off, game-default AF, dithering on,
original colour steps, and original vignette.
Modern: full resolution (100%), TAA, 16x AF, dithering off, Smooth + bias,
and original vignette.
Custom: your own choices; changing individual controls selects it automatically.

AA: Off, FXAA (edge smoothing), or TAA (less shimmering, potentially softer).
AF: Game default or 1x-16x; improves angled textures where sampling allows.
Dithering: toggle the patterned colour noise.
Gradients: Original (stepped), Smooth (interpolated), or Smooth + bias
(interpolated with more of the original palette's warmth).
Vignette: edge darkening, from Off to the original strength at 100%.

Fresh installs preserve your current settings until you choose an option.
Changes save immediately. Old Enhanced preferences become Modern.
The included enabled.txt enables QDGraphics without replacing mods.txt.
For a nonstandard UE4SS working directory, move QDGraphics into its Mods folder.
Optional: ConsoleEnablerMod provides the console (tested with F10); qd_help lists
commands, qd_status shows values, qd_defaults selects Original.

Tested: Steam build 26356968, UE4.27, UE4SS 3.0.1, D3D11. DX12 unverified.
Updates or mods replacing the same three assets may require a rebuild.
Smooth gradients cost additional texture samples; Lua has no polling timer.

UNINSTALL (close the game first)
Remove QueensDomain_Graphics_P.pak and QueensDomain_GraphicsMenu_P.pak from
QueensDomain/Content/Paks, and QDGraphics from UE4SS's Mods folder.
Keep other mods, original game files, and saves. Choose Original before removal
to restore stock resolution behavior. Optional preference reset: delete
%LOCALAPPDATA%\QueensDomain\Saved\QDGraphics.ini and QDGraphics.ini.bak.

UPGRADING EARLY LOCAL TEST BUILDS
Disable ConsoleLab and NoDither, and remove the earlier
QueensDomain_SmoothLUT_P.pak before using this replacement.
