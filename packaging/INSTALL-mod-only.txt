QUEEN'S DOMAIN GRAPHICS — MOD-ONLY

Requires an existing UE4SS installation.

1. Close the game and extract this ZIP somewhere temporary.
2. Copy both files inside PAKs to QueensDomain/Content/Paks.
3. Copy the whole QDGraphics folder into your active UE4SS Mods folder:
   • Flat layout: QueensDomain/Binaries/Win64/Mods
   • Nested layout: QueensDomain/Binaries/Win64/ue4ss/Mods
4. Launch, load your save, then open Options → Video → Graphics preset → Modern.

Follow your existing folder structure. Keep your loader, configuration and other mods. When updating, replace only the mod's files.

Help: https://github.com/jmroon/queens-domain-graphics
